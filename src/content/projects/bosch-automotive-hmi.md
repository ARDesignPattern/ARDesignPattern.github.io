## Overview

Selected design work from an internship at Bosch, exploring visual interface concepts and physical form studies for automotive and motorcycle instrument clusters.

The work focused on the relationship between display layout, information hierarchy, and the physical form of instrument-cluster housings.

## Selected Design Archive

![Motorcycle instrument cluster case design](/images/projects/bosch-automotive-hmi/MotorbikeClusterCaseDesign.png)

### Motorcycle Cluster Concept

Exploration of display proportions, cluster housing forms, and integration with motorcycle cockpit geometry.

![Vehicle instrument cluster interface design](/images/projects/bosch-automotive-hmi/vehicleClusterDesign.png)

### Vehicle Cluster Interface

Interface and layout studies for automotive instrument clusters, including gauge organization, visual hierarchy, and display composition.
