## Overview

Selected interface design work for professional digital music products, including audio effects, instrument interfaces, and mixing-system concepts.

The work explored how complex audio functions could be organized into clear, controllable digital interfaces for musicians and professional users.

## Selected Design Archive

![Digital instrument and audio effect interface concepts](/images/projects/music-tribe-uiux/InstrumentUIDesign.png)

### Instrument & Effects Interfaces

Interface studies for digital instruments and audio effects, exploring control hierarchy, visual feedback, and hardware-inspired interaction patterns.

![WING digital mixing console interface design](/images/projects/music-tribe-uiux/WINGMixerUIDesign.png)

### WING Mixer Interface

Interface concepts for a professional digital mixing environment, focusing on channel organization, monitoring, routing, and efficient access to complex audio controls.
