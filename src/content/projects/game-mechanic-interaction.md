## Balancing Play and Learning

Museum experiences often prioritize learning and interpretation, but playful interaction can make visitors more willing to participate and explore.

I investigated how game mechanics could be integrated into HMD-based museum AR without turning the experience into a conventional game.

The goal was to balance **playfulness, exploration, and learning**.

## My Role

I designed and implemented three HMD-AR interaction concepts based on puzzle, drawing, and action-game mechanics, and evaluated how they influenced engagement, playfulness, and museum exploration.
