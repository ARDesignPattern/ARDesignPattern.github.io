## From Physical Products to Digital Interaction

Before focusing on HCI and XR research, I worked across industrial design, digital interfaces, smart glasses, automotive HMI, and professional audio products.

These projects helped shape the way I approach interaction design today: connecting physical form, digital interfaces, user behavior, and the wider context in which a product is actually used.

---

## ZEISS — Smart Glasses & Interaction Design

**UI/UX Design · Smart Glasses · AR Interaction · Concept Development**

At ZEISS, I worked on a concept exploring how smart glasses and smartphones could support leisure-time exploration and location-based experiences.

My work combined user and market research with interaction design, interface concepts, and AR prototyping.

### My Role

- User and market research
- Interaction concept development
- Smart-glasses interface design
- Mobile companion experience
- AR interaction prototyping
- Visual and interaction design
- Concept presentation and demonstration

The project was also closely connected to my master's thesis and became an important transition from product design toward interaction design and emerging interface technologies.

## Bosch — Automotive HMI & Digital Interface Design

**Automotive HMI · UI/UX · Interface Design · Product Visualization**

At Bosch, I worked on interaction and interface concepts for digital dashboards and motorcycle display systems.

The work explored how information could be structured and presented clearly within highly constrained mobility contexts.

### My Role

- Interface and layout exploration
- Wireframing
- Interaction concepts
- Digital dashboard design
- Motorcycle display concepts
- 3D product visualization
- Visual design support

This experience strengthened my interest in interfaces that connect digital information with physical environments, devices, and real-time user activity.

## Music Tribe — UI/UX for Digital Music Products

**UI/UX · Digital Products · 3D Interface Design · Interaction Design**

At Music Tribe, I worked on user experience and interface design for digital products in the professional music and audio domain.

The work included UX analysis, interface development, and visual exploration for products combining complex functionality with demanding real-time workflows.

### My Role

- UX analysis
- Interface design
- Interaction design
- 3D modeling
- 3D interface exploration
- Product and visual concept development

Working with professional audio products further developed my ability to structure complex systems into understandable and usable interfaces.

## A Broader Design Foundation

Although my later work increasingly moved toward XR, spatial interaction, and HCI research, these professional experiences remain an important part of my design practice.

They established a foundation across:

**Physical Products · Digital Interfaces · Interaction Design · Emerging Technology**

This combination continues to influence how I approach interactive systems today — not only as interfaces on screens, but as experiences situated between people, devices, space, and technology.
