## Overview

This project explores how recurring interaction challenges in head-mounted augmented reality can be transformed into reusable design knowledge.

The work combines interaction design, iterative prototyping, in-situ museum studies, and design research to develop a reusable pattern system for HMD-based AR experiences.

## Role

Interaction design, XR prototyping, user research, study design, data analysis, and pattern development.

## Context

Doctoral research conducted in collaboration with cultural heritage and museum environments in Germany and Europe.
