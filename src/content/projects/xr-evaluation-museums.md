## The Challenge

HMD interaction happens in two places at once: inside the headset and in the physical environment.

This makes real-world XR harder to observe, evaluate, and document than conventional screen-based interaction.

## Making Interaction Observable

I combined the streamed HMD view with direct observation of the user's physical behavior.

This made it possible to understand both:

- what the user sees and does inside XR
- how the user moves and reacts in the real environment

## Field Evaluation

I used structured observation to record interaction success, hesitation, movement, confusion, and assistance without repeatedly interrupting the visitor.

The approach was reused across multiple museum XR studies and different HMD platforms.

## Documenting XR in the Field

HMD experiences are also difficult to communicate through conventional video recording.

I led a small team to plan and capture field demos using participants, external cameras, in-headset recordings, and post-production to show both the digital interaction and its physical context.

## My Role

**Field Study Design · UX Evaluation · XR Documentation · Team Coordination**

I developed and refined practical workflows for studying and communicating embodied XR interaction in real environments.

## Applied in Practice

The workflow was used across different prototypes, exhibits, devices, and museum environments in the **presentXR** and **LoGaCulture** projects.
